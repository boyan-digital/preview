Frequently asked

Before you call

What does it cost to hire you?

Nothing upfront. We work on a contingency fee, which means we're paid only if we recover compensation for you.

How long do I have to file a claim?

For most personal injury cases in California, the statute of limitations is two years from the date of the injury. Some claims, such as those against government agencies, have much shorter deadlines.

Is the consultation confidential?

Yes. Your free consultation is private, and there is no obligation to hire us afterward.

Do you handle cases outside the Bay Area?

We represent clients throughout California from our San Francisco, Los Angeles, Redwood City and Brentwood offices.
